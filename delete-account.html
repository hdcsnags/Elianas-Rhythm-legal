Delete My Account & Data

Eliana's Rhythm lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data — your journal entries and your profile — in two ways.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Eliana's Rhythm → Settings → Account & Data → Delete My Data, confirm, and it's done immediately.

Option 2 — Without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at the address below from the email associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete My Data," and we'll process the deletion of your journal entries, profile, and account within a reasonable time.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your journal entries
  • Your profile
  • Your account sign-in record

Data that isn't tied to your identity — such as Bible study content you may have helped generate, which is cached for all users — is not personal data and is not affected by this request. See our Privacy Policy for details.
