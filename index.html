Eliana's Rhythm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

What this app is

Eliana's Rhythm is a Christian spiritual companion app — Bible reading, an AI companion for conversation and Scripture study, journaling, and prayer tracking.

Data this app collects, and where it goes

Account info. Sign-in is via Google Sign-In and is optional — the app works without an account, with journal/prayer data staying local to your device by default. If you sign in, your email and a user ID are stored in Firebase Authentication.

Journal and prayer entries. Stored locally on your device by default. If you're signed in, these sync to Firestore (Google Cloud) so they're available across your devices.

Conversations with Eliana (the AI companion). When you talk to Eliana or ask a question about a Bible verse, your message is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a response. This can include personal reflections, emotional or spiritual struggles, or anything else you choose to share in conversation — please be aware this content leaves the app and is processed by a third party (OpenAI) to generate the response.

Bible study insights you generate. When you ask Eliana about a specific verse and no cached answer exists yet, your request generates a fresh answer, which is cached in the app's shared Bible-study database (Firestore) so future users benefit from it too — this cached content is the Scripture commentary itself, not tied to your identity in the cache.

Crash and usage data. Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (usage patterns) collect standard diagnostic data to help fix bugs and understand feature usage.

Sensitive content

This app is explicitly religious/spiritual in nature. Conversations, journal entries, and prayer requests may reflect your religious beliefs, mental health, or personal struggles. We treat this content as sensitive: it is not sold, and is not used for advertising — this app shows no ads and has no ad SDKs.

Third parties this data may pass through to provide the app's features:

  • OpenAI — processes conversation text to generate Eliana's responses. Subject to OpenAI's own privacy policy and API data-use terms.
  • Google (Firebase) — authentication, data storage, crash reports, and usage analytics. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

Voice/guided-session features are disabled app-wide in this build — no audio is sent to any voice provider. If that feature is re-enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated before that release ships.

Your choices

  • You can use the app's Bible reading and study features without signing in at all — no account, no cloud sync, no data leaves your device except direct AI-companion conversations and cached verse insights, which happen regardless of sign-in state.
  • You can permanently delete your journal entries, profile, and account at any time from Settings → Account & Data → Delete My Data.

Changes to this policy
