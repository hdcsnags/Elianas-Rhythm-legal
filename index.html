Eliana's Rhythm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What this app is

Eliana's Rhythm is a Christian spiritual companion app: Bible reading and study, an AI companion for conversation about Scripture, journaling, prayer, and short stillness exercises. It is made by an individual developer, not a company. It shows no ads and contains no ad SDKs.

Data this app collects, and where it goes

Account info. Sign-in is via Google Sign-In and is optional. The app works fully without an account; journal and prayer data then stays on your device. If you sign in, your email address and a user ID are stored in Firebase Authentication (Google).

Journal and prayer entries. Stored on your device by default. If you are signed in, they sync to Firestore (Google Cloud) under your user ID so they are available across your devices. They are kept until you delete them or delete your account.

Conversations with Eliana (the AI companion), verse questions, journaling prompts and generated prayers. When you talk to Eliana, ask about a verse, ask for a journaling prompt or ask for a prayer, the text you type is sent through our own relay server (a Firebase Cloud Function) to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the response. This can include personal reflections, emotional or spiritual struggles, or anything else you choose to share. Please be aware that this content leaves the app and is processed by a third party (Anthropic) to generate the response. The relay exists so that no API credentials live on your phone; it does not keep a copy of your messages. Your conversation history is kept on your device, not on our servers.

Bible study insights. When you ask Eliana about a specific verse and no answer exists yet, the generated commentary is cached in the app's shared Bible-study database (Firestore) so future readers benefit from it. The cache holds the commentary itself, the verse reference and the model that wrote it. It does not hold your identity or your question text.

Crash and usage data. Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (which screens and features are used) collect standard diagnostic data, including a device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier, to fix bugs and understand feature use.

What is not collected. No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. Voice and guided-audio sessions are disabled in this release, so no audio is recorded or sent to any voice provider. Stillness exercises play audio bundled with the app. If voice features are enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated before that release ships.

Sensitive content

This app is explicitly religious and spiritual in nature. Conversations, journal entries and prayer requests may reflect your religious beliefs, mental health or personal struggles. We treat this content as sensitive: it is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used to train any model by us.

Third parties this data may pass through to provide the app's features:

  • Anthropic — processes conversation, verse-question, journaling-prompt and prayer text to generate Eliana's responses, via our relay. Subject to Anthropic's privacy policy and its commercial API data terms, under which API inputs and outputs are not used to train Anthropic's models.
  • Google (Firebase) — authentication, data storage, the relay server, crash reports and usage analytics. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for adults and for teens with a parent's permission. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • You can use Bible reading and study without signing in. Without an account there is no cloud sync; the only data that leaves your device is what you type to Eliana and the cached verse insights described above.
  • You can permanently delete your journal entries, prayers, profile and account at any time from Settings → Account & Data → Delete My Data. Deletion removes your Firestore data and your Firebase Authentication record. You can also request deletion by email; see account deletion.

Changes to this policy

When the app's data practices change, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. Material changes, such as enabling voice features, are announced in the app before they take effect.
